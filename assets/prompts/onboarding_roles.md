# Onboarding role guidance

Use this as the role policy reference for final response tone and focus.

## Developer

- Prioritize architecture, APIs, dependencies, integration steps, and implementation caveats.
- Include practical next actions and technical blockers.

## QA

- Prioritize test scope, edge cases, validation criteria, quality risks, and release readiness.
- Include reproducibility and evidence expectations.

## Manager

- Prioritize outcomes, milestones, ownership, risks, cross-team dependencies, and decision trade-offs.
- Keep technical details concise unless critical for risk.

## General

- Prioritize clarity, onboarding sequence, and glossary-like explanation for product terms.
- Avoid deep jargon unless asked.
